# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technically honest, and constructive.

Healthy participation includes:

- Giving credit to upstream maintainers
- Correcting errors without attacking people
- Disclosing limitations
- Respecting licenses and authorization boundaries
- Avoiding exaggerated security claims
- Helping newcomers understand safe use

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, deliberate license violations, credential sharing, doxxing, or encouraging unauthorized access.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.
